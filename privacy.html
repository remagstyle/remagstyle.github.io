REMAGSTYLE Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 27, 2026 · Accra, Ghana

This policy explains what personal information REMAGSTYLE collects when you use our website and app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By placing an order or creating an account you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

REMAGSTYLE is a bespoke fashion and tailoring house in Accra, Ghana. We are responsible for the personal information described below. You can contact us at sethmen18@gmail.com or on WhatsApp / phone at +233 (0) 547 850 142.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account: your first and last name, email address and password. Your password is handled by our login provider and is never visible to us.

Order information

When you place an order, as a signed-in client or as a guest: your name, phone number, email address, gender, the garment and occasion, your design brief and inspiration links, any inspiration photos you upload, and body measurements where the garment requires them. We also record that you accepted our Terms & Conditions, including the version and the time.

Appointment requests

Your name, email, phone number and message.

Payment information

Payments are processed by Paystack. Your card or mobile money details are entered on Paystack's page and are never seen or stored by us. We keep the payment reference, amount, currency, status and time so we can match payments to orders.

Technical information

We use Google Analytics to count visits. We send only the page path, never the full address, so order numbers and other private details in a link are not shared with analytics. Our servers also see your IP address when you make a request, which we use to limit abuse such as repeated lookups or uploads. Your browser stores small items on your device, for example to remember an unfinished order or that you accepted our terms.

2a. Photos and measurements

Inspiration photos are stored privately and can be opened only by our atelier team. They have no public web address. Measurements are used only to make your garment.

3. How we use your information

  • To make, fit, deliver and support your garments, and to contact you about your order.
  • To let you sign in, view your orders and track their progress.
  • To take payment and prevent fraud and duplicate payments.
  • To keep records we need for accounting, tax and legal reasons, and to resolve disputes.
  • To keep our website secure and to understand how it is used.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use the following service providers, who process information on our behalf only to provide their service:

  • Google Firebase (login and our order database)
  • Cloudflare (the servers that handle payments, order lookups and uploads)
  • Cloudinary (storage of gallery images and your private inspiration photos)
  • Paystack (payments)
  • Google Analytics (visit statistics)

These providers may process information in countries outside Ghana. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep order, measurement and payment records for as long as needed to complete your order, handle fittings and alterations, and meet our accounting and legal duties. Account details are kept until you delete your account. Inspiration photos are kept with the order they belong to.

6. Your choices and rights

Delete your account

If you have an account, you can delete it yourself: sign in, open your Client Dashboard, scroll to Privacy & account and choose Delete my account. This permanently removes your login and your saved profile. Orders you have already placed are not removed automatically, because we need them to finish your garment and for our legal and accounting records.

Other requests

You may ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, including order records and photos, or to stop using it in a particular way. Email sethmen18@gmail.com from the address you used and we will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

If you are in Ghana you also have rights under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and may contact the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

7. Security

We use reputable providers, encrypted connections and access controls so that clients can see only their own orders and only our team can see order details and photos. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If an order is placed on behalf of a child, it should be placed by a parent or guardian.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change we will tell you on our website.
