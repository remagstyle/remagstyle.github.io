REMAGSTYLE Terms & Conditions and Store Policies

Effective Date: October 20, 2026 · Accra, Ghana

1. Bespoke & Custom Garment Orders

1.1 Consultations & Design Finalization

All custom haute couture, bridal, and luxury garments require an initial consultation to confirm design concepts, textile selections, embellishments, and measurements. Once a design illustration and fabric swatch selection are approved by the client, any subsequent changes to the design may incur additional charges and extend the estimated completion timeline.

1.2 Measurements & Fitting Responsibility

Clients are strongly encouraged to undergo in-person measurement sessions conducted by our design team. If a client provides their own measurements (for remote/international orders), REMAGSTYLE is not liable for fit issues resulting from inaccurate self-measurements. Subsequent body shape or size changes (weight loss or gain) after measurements are taken may require paid alterations.

2. Payment Terms & Pricing

2.1 Payment Schedule

Deposit: A non-refundable 60% deposit is required to begin fabric sourcing, pattern development, and production.
Final Balance: The remaining 40% balance is due upon the final fitting or prior to garment dispatch/pickup. No completed garment will be released or dispatched without full settlement of the invoice balance.

2.2 Payment Methods

We accept bank transfers, mobile money, and credit/debit card payments through our approved channels.

3. Production Timelines & Emergency/Express Orders

Standard Turnaround: Standard production for custom bespoke garments takes 3 to 6 weeks, depending on the complexity of hand-beading, embroidery, and structural detailing.
Rush Orders: Orders requested within a shorter timeframe than the standard lead time are subject to an Express Rush Fee (typically 20%–30% of the total cost), subject to production capacity.

4. Fittings, Alterations & Pickups

4.1 Fitting Appointments

Custom orders include up to two (2) standard fitting sessions. Clients are expected to bring the exact footwear and undergarments intended to be worn with the garment to all fitting sessions to ensure accurate hem lengths and structural fit.

4.2 Post-Delivery Alterations

Free minor adjustments are provided within 7 days of garment collection or delivery, provided the design was not altered from the original agreement. Alteration requests made after 7 days will incur standard alteration fees.

5. Cancellation & Refund Policy

5.1 Bespoke & Custom Orders

Non-Refundable Deposit: Due to the custom, made-to-order nature of our garments and immediate commitment of materials, deposits are strictly non-refundable. If an order is canceled after garment construction or fabric cutting has commenced, the client remains responsible for payment proportional to the work completed up to that point.

5.2 Catalog / Ready-to-Wear Items

Unworn, unwashed, and undamaged items with original tags intact may be exchanged within 7 days of purchase for store credit or another item of equal value. No cash refunds are issued.

6. Shipping, Delivery & International Orders

Delivery Costs: Clients are responsible for all shipping, delivery, and courier fees unless otherwise explicitly agreed in writing.
Customs & Import Duties: For international orders, any customs duties, taxes, or clearance fees levied by the destination country are the sole responsibility of the recipient.
Delays: REMAGSTYLE is not liable for shipping delays caused by third-party courier services, customs processing, or force majeure events.

7. Intellectual Property & Photography Consent

7.1 Intellectual Property

All original garment sketches, patterns, photography, and digital marketing materials created by REMAGSTYLE remain the exclusive intellectual property of REMAGSTYLE. Reproduction or commercial duplication without written consent is strictly prohibited.

7.2 Media & Photography

REMAGSTYLE reserves the right to photograph finished garments and fittings for archival, portfolio, and marketing purposes. If a client requires confidentiality (e.g., secret wedding dresses or unreleased event wear), please inform us prior to the commencement of production so an NDA/embargo date can be applied.

8. Customer Conduct & Communication

We strive to provide an exemplary luxury experience for all clients and expect respectful communication with our staff, tailors, and customer service team. REMAGSTYLE reserves the right to refuse service or cancel contracts in cases of abusive behavior or breach of these terms.
